Audible Pages

Turns any text-based PDF into a smooth, human-sounding audio reading, or a two-host conversation about it. Play it in the browser or download the whole thing as audio. Built with React 19, TanStack Start, TypeScript, Tailwind and shadcn/ui, with Supabase for auth, storage and the database. Text is extracted from PDFs in the browser with pdf.js, then split into passages, voiced with Gemini text-to-speech, and stitched into one continuous WAV.
